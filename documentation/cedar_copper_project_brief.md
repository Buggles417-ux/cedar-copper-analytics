# Cedar & Copper Analytics Portfolio Project Brief

## Business problem

Cedar & Copper is a fictional omnichannel kitchenware retailer selling through physical stores, its website, and a mobile app. Leadership lacks a unified view of sales, customer, product, channel, and return performance. This project analyzes the company’s data to identify the primary drivers of revenue, customer value, product performance, and operational issues so leadership can make better business decisions.

## Project objective

The objective is to create an end-to-end analytics solution that combines SQL analysis, KPI definitions, data validation, and Power BI reporting. The final deliverable will provide leadership with a clear view of overall performance and actionable insights about customers, products, sales channels, and returns.

## Intended audience

The primary audience is Cedar & Copper’s executive and business leadership team, including leaders responsible for sales, e-commerce, merchandising, marketing, and customer strategy. The analysis will focus on clear business interpretation and recommended actions rather than technical complexity.

## Analysis scope

The project will analyze:

- Overall sales and revenue performance
- Customer segments, purchasing frequency, and customer value
- Product and category performance
- Store, website, and mobile-app sales channels
- Returns and potential product or operational issues
- Marketing performance where the available campaign data supports reliable conclusions

## Data

The project uses a fictional SQLite database covering January 2024 through December 2025. It includes 10,000 customers, 30,000 orders, 68,292 order-item records, 80 products, 25 stores, 60 marketing campaigns, and 3,861 return records. The data supports analysis across physical stores, the website, and the mobile app.

## Key business questions

- How are revenue, orders, and average order value performing over time?
- Which customer segments generate the greatest value?
- Which products and categories drive revenue, unit sales, and margin?
- How does performance differ across stores, the website, and the mobile app?
- Which products or categories have unusually high return activity?
- Where should leadership focus to improve performance?

## Planned KPIs

- Net revenue
- Completed orders
- Units sold
- Average order value
- Net margin and margin percentage
- Purchasing customers
- Orders per customer
- Revenue per customer
- Return rate
- Revenue and orders by sales channel

## Final deliverables

- A documented SQLite database and data model
- Curated SQL analyses with findings and assumptions
- A KPI dictionary
- Documented data-validation and quality checks
- A three-page Power BI report
- Final business findings and recommendations
- A concise README with dashboard screenshots
- A GitHub repository containing the finished portfolio materials

## Definition of finished

Cedar & Copper will be considered finished when the SQL analyses run correctly, KPI definitions are documented, Power BI totals reconcile to SQL, the three report pages communicate a clear business story, and the final findings and recommendations are presented in a concise README with usable screenshots. Additional analysis or technical complexity will not be added unless it directly strengthens the final business story.
